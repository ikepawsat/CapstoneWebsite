B.A.A. Boston Marathon Cutoff and Qualification Standards History

Below you’ll find a short explanation of how the cutoffs work, a compact history of previous cutoff times, and recent application & acceptance numbers.

How the Cutoff Works

The Boston Athletic Association (B.A.A.) accepts a target field size (typically ~24,000 in recent years). When more qualified runners apply than the B.A.A. can accept, applicants are ranked by how much faster than their age-group qualifying time they ran. The fastest qualifiers are accepted until the field is full. The last accepted athlete sets the cutoff, measured as minutes:seconds faster than the published qualifying standards.

Downhill Course Adjustment Policy

Starting with registration for the 2027 Boston Marathon, qualifying results from any course with a net-downhill of 1,500 ft (457.2 m) or more will receive an adjustment (“index”) when submitted.

  • 1,500–2,999 ft downhill: +5:00 minutes adjustment
  • 3,000–5,999 ft downhill: +10:00 minutes adjustment
  • 6,000 ft or more: Not eligible for Boston qualification
